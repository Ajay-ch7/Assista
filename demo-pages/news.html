River levels rise after record rain

By Meera Iyer. Published 4 October 2026.

The Kaveri rose by two metres overnight after the heaviest October rain in forty years. The district office has closed the old bridge and asked people living near the bank to move to higher ground.

What to do

Relief camps are open at the town hall and the government school. Drinking water is available at both. The helpline number is 1077.

Outlook

More rain is expected on Tuesday. Water levels should fall by the weekend.
