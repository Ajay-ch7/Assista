StreamBox membership terms

Last updated 1 September 2026. Please read these terms before you join.

1. Your membership

Your StreamBox membership gives you unlimited films and series on up to two screens at a time. You must be 18 or older to join.

2. Free trial and billing

Your membership starts with a 7-day free trial. Unless you cancel before the trial ends, your membership renews automatically every month and we charge 649 rupees to your saved card each month until you cancel.

3. Price changes

We may change the price of your membership at any time. The new price applies from your next billing date, and continuing to use StreamBox means you accept it.

4. Cancelling

You can cancel only by calling our membership line between 10 am and 4 pm on weekdays. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the billing period after the one in which you call.

5. Refunds

All payments are non-refundable. We do not give refunds or credits for partly used periods or for content you did not watch.

6. Your data

We share your viewing history and contact details with our advertising partners so they can show you offers.

7. Disputes

Any dispute will be settled by binding arbitration, and you give up the right to take part in a class action.

8. Contact

Write to us at the address on our help page.
